# ACT.IN.ART. — Slide-by-slide script with speaker notes

Use this as a ready-made speaking outline. Each slide includes suggested on-slide bullets and matching speaker notes.  
This version assumes a 12–15 minute pitch. You can shorten by combining slides 6–10.

---

## Slide 1 — Title
**On slide**
- Indiana Art Activist Directory  
- ACT.IN.ART. (Activism Indiana Artistry)  
- A living, local engine for civic imagination

**Speaker notes**
Introduce ACT.IN.ART. as a statewide directory of activist artists and artworks designed to inspire civic engagement through art. Emphasize that it is living and community-supported.

---

## Slide 2 — The problem
**On slide**
- Indiana activist art is widespread  
- Documentation is scattered  
- Context is easy to lose  
- New audiences can’t find the work

**Speaker notes**
Explain that powerful work exists across the state, but without a shared home, discovery and education remain fragmented.

---

## Slide 3 — The opportunity
**On slide**
- A directory that also “activates”  
- Local-first learning  
- Community growth loop  
- Reusable public data

**Speaker notes**
Frame ACT.IN.ART. as a modern cultural infrastructure project, not just a list.

---

## Slide 4 — One-sentence mission
**On slide**
- Inspire change in Indiana through art  
- Influence present-day thinking  
- Make activist art visible + contextual

**Speaker notes**
Deliver the mission cleanly and connect it to public, education, and partnership value.
